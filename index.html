FAQ

Questions, answered

The things every renter asks before booking.

What documents do I need to rent a car?

Tourists need a passport, a visit visa, a driving licence from their home country and an international driving permit. UAE residents need an Emirates ID and a UAE driving licence.

What is the minimum age to rent?

21 years for most cars, and 25 years for supercars and high-performance models. Your licence must have been held for at least one year.

Is a deposit required?

No. There is no security deposit on our rentals. Your card is only charged for the rental itself.

How many kilometres are included?

250 km per day is included in every rental. Extra kilometres are charged per km, and the rate is confirmed before you book.

Is insurance included?

Yes, insurance is included in the daily price. Full cover options are available on request.

Where do you deliver?

Anywhere in Dubai: hotels, homes, offices and Dubai International Airport. Delivery is free inside Dubai and usually takes around 30 minutes.
